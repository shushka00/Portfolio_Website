## 4 · Voice rules

| Rule |
|---|
| Short declarative sentences in sentence case and present tense. Start with a verb where you can. |
| Business words carry the message: adopt, activate, convert, deploy, pay for, revenue. Show the number instead of saying "strategic". |
| Every figure gets its measure and baseline when space allows. |
| Keep credit exact. "I design" for craft, "helped" or "the team" for shared results. |
| No em or en dashes, no arrows, no hype words, no "I'm a", no greeting opener. Join label pairs with " · ". |
| Test every line: would a person write this? |
| Headline 8 words, lede 25, fold 80. Over budget means cut. |
